Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Wally is a personal automation assistant operated by Sean Goh for his own use. It is not open for public sign-ups and does not connect other visitors’ Google accounts. This policy covers Wally’s Gmail and Google Calendar integration and this public website.

Data Wally can access

With the owner’s Google authorisation, Wally can access relevant Gmail message headers, senders, recipients, subjects, message bodies and attachments; create and manage drafts; and send email. It can read calendar event details and availability and create events, including titles, times, descriptions, locations and attendees where relevant.

The integration requests Gmail read-only, compose and send permissions, and Google Calendar event permissions. The permissions granted may be broader than the particular operation being performed. OAuth access and refresh tokens allow authorised access without Wally asking for the owner’s Google password.

How data is used

Wally uses this data to assist the owner with finding and understanding messages, preparing replies, carrying out authorised communications, organising tasks and managing the calendar. Relevant content can be included in task context, summaries and workflow records.

Processing and sharing

Google processes the Gmail and Calendar API requests. When the owner uses AI-assisted functions, relevant messages, event details, prompts and tool results can be sent to OpenAI to produce responses or assist with the requested task. The implementation supports server-stored responses for continuing tool-assisted conversations.

When configured and used by the owner, selected task details or derived records can also be passed to connected tools, such as Telegram for notifications and Notion for task records. Email recipients and calendar attendees receive information through the communications the owner authorises. These are task-related uses, not public publication of the owner’s mailbox or calendar.

Wally does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models. Wally’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Wally can retain conversations, summaries, selected message or event content, attachments, task records and operational or audit logs on the owner’s device. Selected local records can also be copied to the owner’s private iCloud Drive when backup is enabled. Credentials are held in private runtime configuration or credential storage, separately from this public website.

There is no fixed automatic deletion period for Wally’s local records: they remain until the owner removes them. Copies held by connected services are subject to those services’ settings, retention rules and deletion controls. This policy does not promise immediate erasure of provider-held copies.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can remove Wally’s Google access at Google Account connections. This prevents further authorised access once revocation takes effect; it does not itself delete previously saved records.

For questions or deletion requests, email gohsusean@gmail.com. Deletion is handled manually by the operator and includes identifying applicable local records, backups and connected-service copies. Wally does not currently offer a self-service deletion page. Removing Wally’s copies does not automatically remove original Gmail messages, sent email or calendar events from Google or recipients’ accounts.

This public website

These information pages contain no sign-in flow, forms, analytics scripts or advertising trackers. They do not collect Gmail or Calendar content or OAuth credentials. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following external links or emailing the operator uses the relevant provider’s services.

Contact and changes

Operator: Sean Goh. Support and privacy contact: gohsusean@gmail.com. This policy will be updated when Wally’s data practices change; the revision date appears above.
