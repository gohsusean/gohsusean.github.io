Gmail & Google Calendar

A little help with
the everyday.

Wally is Sean Goh’s personal automation assistant for managing email, calendar tasks and everyday workflows.

Wally is used by its owner. This website provides public information about the assistant; it does not offer account registration or connect visitors’ Google accounts.

What Wally does

  • Finds and reads relevant Gmail messages and attachments to help the owner understand and organise tasks.
  • Prepares email drafts and sends messages through the owner’s authorised workflows.
  • Reads Google Calendar events, checks availability and creates events for the owner.

Access depends on the Google permissions the owner grants and the workflows they use. Wally’s AI-assisted functions can process relevant task context through OpenAI.

Your account stays in your control

Google authorisation is handled by Google. This public website does not ask for passwords, receive OAuth tokens or access Gmail or Calendar data.

Read the Wally privacy policy for information about the assistant’s data access, processing, storage and deletion. Google access can be removed through Google Account connections.
